# Research Log

## Purpose

This document records observations, hypotheses, questions, and conceptual developments that emerge during the research process.

Unlike formal papers, entries may be incomplete, exploratory, or speculative.

# Entry 1

## Humans May Perceive Patterns Before Cells

Observation:

Certain Karnaugh-map patterns appear to be recognized immediately.

Examples:

- Checkerboards
- Symmetry
- Repetition

Hypothesis:

Human perception may operate as:

Pattern Hypothesis
→ Exception Detection
→ Structure Confirmation

rather than:

Cell Reading
→ Pattern Discovery

Potential Significance:

This may explain why some visual representations accelerate understanding.

# Entry 2

## Difference → Attention

Observation:

Not all patterns attract attention equally.

Examples:

- Symmetry
- Contrast
- Repetition
- Anomaly

Hypothesis:

Difference may be an attention-generating mechanism.

Proposed Progression:

Difference
→ Attention
→ Structure Discovery

Research Question:

Why do some differences stand out while others remain unnoticed?

# Entry 3

## Structure Discoverer Hypothesis

Observation:

AI often performs computation, organization, and summarization.

Humans often generate questions and identify meaningful structures.

Hypothesis:

In the AI era, human comparative advantage may increasingly lie in:

- Structure Discovery
- Question Generation
- Meaning Attribution

Potential Research Question:

Is the role of humans shifting from calculator to structure discoverer?
