Concept genealogy

How the ideas actually arrived

The real order of discovery, not a logical reconstruction.

  1. Pattern

    First observation: alternating checkerboard patterns for XOR/XNOR on Karnaugh maps.

  2. Layer structure

    Hamming-weight layers govern where the patterns of symmetric functions sit.

  3. Equivalence and structural invariance

    Observation: variable rearrangements preserve structural features of the picture across map re-orderings.

  4. Structure Recognition Theory

    The meta-question: why do some structures trigger research inquiry while others go unnoticed?

  5. Human-AI collaboration model

    Division of cognitive labor: humans discover structure intuitively; AI systematically widens the space of explanations.

  6. Boolean function space theory (archived)

    A former mathematical frontier, now superseded by Paper 7: gate-generated subspaces and Post's lattice.

  7. Standalone Papers 7 and 8 (current)

    Group-theoretic classification of 4-variable Boolean function spaces (7) and structural thinking with human-AI research collaboration (8). Each can be read without the earlier papers.

About

Jonghun Choi · 최종훈

Independent researcher and second-year student at Inha Technical College. The work sits between Boolean structure analysis, cognition and long-term human-AI collaboration. Author of Structure Recognition Theory (SRT) and the Human-AI Research Continuity Theory (HARCT).

The program is AI-assisted. Humans notice structure and pose the questions; AI connects concepts and widens the space of explanations. That division of labor is itself under study (Papers 5 and 8).
