Program Overview

Abstract & Methodology

This portfolio documents a long-term research program exploring the cognitive and mathematical mechanisms of research generation. Beginning with specific observations of Karnaugh map visual patterns, the program developed through three empirical case studies into an integrated theoretical framework: Structure Recognition Theory (SRT).

The program adheres to Architecture B (Empirical Foundation Model): three empirical papers ground the theoretical integration in Paper 4, with Paper 5 addressing human-AI collaboration as a methodological meta-layer for distributed research continuity.

Epistemological Journey

Concept Evolution & Timeline

How simple visual observations evolved into an interconnected theoretical program.

1. Karnaugh Map Pattern Discovery
Initial empirical noticing of alternating checkerboard structures and visual symmetries in XOR/XNOR maps, raising the question of why certain visual representations trigger intuitive insights.
2. Symmetric Boolean Functions & Hamming Layers
Generalization from 2D visual maps to n-dimensional Boolean hypercubes. Identification of concentric ring structures invariant to input permutations.
3. Variable Rearrangement & Topological Invariance
Proving that the underlying information structure remains invariant regardless of how variables are ordered, confirming structure as an objective property of the function space.
4. Formalization of Structure Recognition Theory (SRT)
Synthesizing observational data into a general theory of discovery. Modeling the cognitive transition from pattern noticing to question generation.
5. Unified Synthesis (Papers 7 & 8)
Two-track synthesis: Track A unifies D₄ and S₄ group-theoretic actions into a comprehensive classification of Boolean function spaces (P7). Track B establishes the integrated theory of human structural thinking and human-AI research continuity for future education (P8).
