Research Program — Structure Recognition

This portfolio is the program navigation hub for a unified long-term research program investigating how humans discover structure, assign meaning to it, and generate research questions.

The program began with specific Boolean function phenomena observed in Karnaugh maps and has expanded into a broader meta-theoretical framework — Structure Recognition Theory (SRT) — with branches into human-AI collaboration and AI education.
