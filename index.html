A long-term investigation into how humans discover structure and generate research

How do humans come to see something new?

Abstract

This portfolio documents a long-term research program investigating how humans discover structure, assign meaning to it, and generate research questions. Beginning with specific observations of Karnaugh map visual patterns, the program developed through three empirical case studies into a theoretical framework — Structure Recognition Theory (SRT) — addressing the cognitive mechanisms of research generation and human-AI collaborative discovery.

The program follows Architecture B (Empirical Foundation Model): three empirical papers ground the theoretical integration in Paper 4, with Paper 5 addressing human-AI collaboration as a methodological meta-layer.
