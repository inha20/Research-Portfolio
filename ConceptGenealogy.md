# Concept Genealogy

## Introduction

This document records the evolution of concepts that emerged throughout the research program.

The goal is not merely to define concepts, but to document how one concept generated another.

---

# Lineage 1

Pattern → Structure → Structure Discovery

## Pattern

Initial observations often begin with repeated, symmetric, anomalous, or visually salient phenomena.

Examples:

- Checkerboards
- Symmetry
- Repetition
- Ring structures

↓

## Structure

A pattern becomes a structure when relationships between elements are recognized.

Examples:

- Weight layers
- Symmetric Boolean functions
- Karnaugh-map organization

↓

## Structure Discovery

Research increasingly shifted from studying structures themselves to understanding how humans discover structures.

Central Question:

Why do humans notice some structures but ignore others?

---

# Lineage 2

Difference → Attention → Meaning

## Difference

Certain features stand out against a background.

Examples:

- Symmetry
- Anomaly
- Contrast
- Repetition

↓

## Attention

Differences attract attention.

Hypothesis:

Attention may be a prerequisite for structure discovery.

↓

## Meaning

Structures become meaningful when they are perceived as non-random.

Research Question:

Why do some structures feel significant?

---

# Lineage 3

Meaning → Question → Research

## Meaning

Meaning attribution creates curiosity.

↓

## Question

Questions emerge when meaningful structures are encountered.

Examples:

- Why is this pattern present?
- Why is this invariant?
- Why does this symmetry exist?

↓

## Research

Research begins when questions become systematic investigations.

Proposed Progression:

Pattern → Attention → Structure → Meaning → Question → Research

---

# Lineage 4

Karnaugh Map → Weight Layer → Invariance → Structure Recognition

## Karnaugh Map

Initial visual observations of Boolean functions.

↓

## Weight Layer

Recognition that symmetric Boolean functions can be interpreted through Hamming-weight layers.

↓

## Invariance

Recognition that certain structural properties remain unchanged under transformations.

↓

## Structure Recognition

Shift from studying structures to studying recognition itself.

↓

## Structure Recognition Theory

Current theoretical framework integrating:

- Pattern

- Meaning
- Question Generation
- Research Worthiness
