## 5. Current Theory — Structure Recognition Theory (SRT)

### Core Research Questions (Two-Level)

**Program Level:**
> How do humans come to see something new?

**Theory Level:**
> How do humans detect explanatory significance?

### Research Generation Framework
```
Difference
 → Attention
 → Structure
 → Meaning
 → Research Worthiness
 → Question
 → Research
```

### Concept Evolution Cycle (integrated in SRT v0.2)
```
Research → Concept → Expanded Perception → New Observations → New Questions → New Research
```

### Hypotheses (H1–H10)
- **H1** Structure Discoverer Hypothesis: Human advantage lies in structure discovery
- **H2** Representation Transformation: Different representations reveal different structures
- **H3** Attention Filter: Not all patterns receive investigation
- **H4** Significance Filter: Implicit filter evaluates "worth investigating"
- **H5** Explanation Anticipation: Humans anticipate explanation before finding it
- **H6** Explanatory Significance: Research begins from detecting explanatory potential
- **H7** H1 Mature: Human core role = explanation opportunity detection
- **H8** Concept-as-Lens: Concepts function as observation lenses *(통합 확장 가설 — SRT v0.3 승격)*
- **H9** Concept Evolution: Research generates concepts; concepts generate further research *(통합 확장 가설 — SRT v0.3 승격)*
- **H10** Generative Concept: Concepts differ in their generativity *(통합 확장 가설 — SRT v0.3 승격)*

All hypotheses are **exploratory**. None are established conclusions.

### Core Question Hierarchy (Paper 4)

Level 0 (Observation) → Level 1 (Structure) → Level 2 (Discovery) → Level 3 (Significance)  
→ Level 4 (Question Generation) → Level 5 (Explanation) → Level 6 (Concept)  
→ Level 7 (Expanded Perception) → Level 8 (New Discovery)

Full hierarchy documented in: `4StructureRecognitionTheory-main/theory/CoreQuestions.md` v2.0

## 8. Human-AI Collaboration Protocol

### The Observed Division of Labor
```
Human Researcher:
  - Notices unusual phenomena
  - Detects anomalies
  - Generates research questions
  - Selects direction of inquiry
  - Evaluates explanatory significance

AI Collaborator (ANTIGRAVITY):
  - Connects concepts across domains
  - Suggests frameworks and structures
  - Expands explanation space
  - Organizes and documents
  - Maintains continuity between sessions
```

### Workflow Rules for AI Collaborators
1. **Preserve** all existing filenames unless explicitly asked to rename
2. **Preserve** discovery history — never delete historical material
3. **Prefer** incremental improvements over full rewrites
4. **Document** all significant decisions in CHANGELOG or handover files
5. **Avoid** merging Papers 1–3 into a single paper
6. **Avoid** forcing theoretical frameworks prematurely
7. **Prefer** conceptual organization over producing more content
8. **이미지 생성 규칙 (ImageStandardizationGuide Rev.2 적용):** 연구자가 직접 PowerPoint/Draw.io/손 그림 스캔으로 제작; **AI 이미지 생성 도구 및 AI가 작성한 코드로 생성한 그림 절대 금지**
