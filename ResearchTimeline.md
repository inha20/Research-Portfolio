# Research Timeline

## Elementary School

### Multiplication Table

One of the earliest experiences of structure recognition.

Observations:

- Repeated addition creates predictable numerical patterns.
- Each multiplication table contains its own internal structure.
- Relationships exist between different tables.

This period marks the first awareness that mathematical objects may contain hidden patterns rather than isolated facts.

---

## Middle School

### Algebraic Identities

Structure discovered through geometric interpretation.

Observations:

- Algebraic expansions can be represented by rectangular areas.
- Symbolic expressions correspond to visual structures.
- Tables and area models reveal hidden regularities.

### Euler Characteristic

Observation:

- Polyhedra satisfy the relation:

V − E + F = 2

This suggested that different shapes may share a common invariant structure.

---

## High School

### Korean CSAT Nonfiction Reading

Observation:

- Complex texts often possess logical and structural organization.
- Understanding structure can be more important than memorizing details.

### Function Construction

Observation:

- Multiple conditions can be integrated into a single coherent structure.
- Mathematical creativity often involves structural synthesis.

### Genetics and Pascal's Triangle

Observation:

- Genetic inheritance patterns correspond to Pascal's Triangle.
- The similarity appears to emerge naturally from independent combinations.

### Symmetric Boolean Functions

Observation:

- Certain Boolean functions remain unchanged under variable exchange.
- Symmetry became a recurring theme.

---

## University

### Karnaugh Maps

Observation:

- Boolean functions can be interpreted visually.
- Large-scale patterns become visible immediately.
- Checkerboards, symmetry, repetition, and grouping appear as meaningful structures.

Research Topics:

- Pattern Recognition
- XOR/XNOR Checkerboards
- Visual Interpretation Methods

### Weight Layer Structures

Observation:

- Symmetric Boolean functions can be understood through Hamming Weight layers.
- Visual patterns correspond to layer combinations.

### Variable Rearrangement Invariance

Observation:

- Many structures remain unchanged despite variable rearrangement.
- Structural invariance emerged as a central research theme.
